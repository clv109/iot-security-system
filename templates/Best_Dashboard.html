System User Guide

1. Arming & Disarming

Use the System Controls panel to manage camera activity.

  • ARM SYSTEM: Starts the camera and detection algorithms.
  • DISARM: Stops the camera process immediately.

2. Managing Users

To add a new authorized person:

  1. Click the Add button in the Authorized Personnel panel.
  2. Enter the person's full name (e.g., "John Doe").
  3. Upload at least 5 clear photos of their face.
  4. Click "Register". The system will re-train its model automatically.

3. Quiet Hours

Quiet Hours define a strict security period. Any detection during this time (even if it looks like a known person but fails strict validation) may trigger enhanced alerts.

Use the dropdowns to set the Start and End times, then click Update Schedule.

4. Model Training Parameters

Please ensure entirety of the face is clearly visible in all shots. The most optmized images for training a highly accurate model will include photos in different lighting conditions (avoid harsh shadows covering parts of the face), facing the camera directly with different facial expressions, horizational rotations of the face in many different angles left and right, vertical rotations of the face up and down. Submitting images that obstruct facial features will greatly hinder the performance of the facial recognition model.

Click add on the top right to get started.
